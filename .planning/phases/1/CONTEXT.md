# Phase 1: The accounting the trace still misses - Context

Gathered: 2026-08-12
Feeds: /cad-plan 1

## Scope boundary

In: A per-step coordinator marker event in the lifecycle family, written by the
coordinator at the steps it can name, carrying no token figure; its consumption
by `/cad-report`'s record-health line and by a `trace suggest` rule; verbatim's
phase-1 `trace.jsonl` committed as a test fixture; and a spend gate in
`context.md` that decides whether to buy the `cad-assumptions-analyzer` pass
BEFORE the `route.mjs resolve` that would bracket it, with a conversational
fallback on the skip arm. Plus the surfaces those two changes force:
`lib/trace.mjs`'s exported lifecycle vocabulary and the producer census that
reads it, `self-verify`'s flag-lint table, `report.md` and `context.md`
weight-budget re-pins, `skills/cad-context/SKILL.md`, the four `DOCS-CLAIMS.md`
rows for `context.md` that this phase's line shifts touch, and the
`docs/EVIDENCE.md` claim the fixture falsifies.

Out: No fifth event family (D-02). No token figure on any coordinator event,
ever (D-03). No computed/scored spend gate and no splitting framework (D-09,
D-11). `size_check` does not move (D-09). The four open `trace.mjs` defects
CAPTURE.md records - the `__proto__` role key, terminal-vs-dispatch role
validation, `recorded` counted per event, and `correlationId` colliding on an
unchanged HEAD - are NOT in this phase; they are pre-existing and none is a
blocker for the marker work.

Deferred: None.

Plan shape: multiple plans, same phase - /cad-plan breaks it down. The two
requirements share almost no surface: TRC-01 lives in the trace library and its
two readers, SIZ-01 lives in `context.md` and its skill. Sequencing the trace
half first lets it land green before the gate half restructures the `analyze`
step.

## Durable decisions

- D-01 (Marker shape): The marker is a per-step lifecycle event carrying the step name and its timestamp, and NOTHING computed - `/cad-report` derives the coordinator residue by subtracting paired bracket spans from the wall span. Evidence: measured 2026-08-12 by pairing `(corr, phase, plan)` over `/data/code/verbatim/.planning/trace.jsonl` phase 1 (28 events, 8 paired brackets): wall span 178.6 min, bracket sum 105.5 min, coordinator residue 73.2 min = 41%; largest single gap 38.7 min at `cad-assumptions-analyzer` return -> `cad-planner` dispatch. Pairing rule at `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:344-401`. A marker carrying its own elapsed field gives one quantity two sources, and they disagree the first time a bracket is unpaired - the fixture already has one such bracket (the 12:24:57 `cad-reviewer` dispatch). Consumption-only with no producer was rejected because the residue then stays phase-wide and never says which step spent it, which is the whole of TRC-01's "per-step".
- D-02 (Vocabulary): The marker is a fifth EXPORTED lifecycle name in `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs`, alongside `DISPATCH`, `TERMINAL` and `ANCHOR` - not a fifth family, not a reuse of `outcome`. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:93-104` - `ANCHOR` (`phase_start`) is already a non-worker lifecycle event that pairs with nothing and carries no `--role`, and `:88-92` states the names are exported so the producer census reads the renderer's real vocabulary rather than a copy; `cadence-core/bin/trace.test.mjs:807-813`, `:882-893`. A fifth family writes fine and counts nowhere - `FAMILIES` is validated at the seam (`:86`) while `renderTrace`'s `counts` is a hardcoded four-key literal gated by `hasOwnProperty` (`:309`, `:354`) - and would force a five-surface cascade (`progress.md:95`, `skills/cad-progress/SKILL.md:24`, `DOCS-CLAIMS.md:600` PROGRESS-14, `PROJECT.md:97`). The `outcome` family holds GATE outcomes (`lib/trace.mjs:4`); a step marker is not one.
- D-03 (No fabricated figure): No coordinator event carries a token figure of any kind. `--tokens` stays sourced from a SUBAGENT's return metadata only. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:35-69` (the TOKEN PROVENANCE header: tokens are "read off the HOST's subagent return metadata at the moment the worker returns. Cadence adds no hook, no seam and no capture mechanism"), plus a grep of every `cadence-core/bin/*.mjs` and `bin/lib/*.mjs` for `usage|input_tokens|total_tokens|context window` returning only unrelated `reason: 'usage'` envelopes. A guessed figure would land in `trace suggest` R5's share denominator (`cadence-core/bin/lib/trace-suggest.mjs:153-168`), which `lib/trace.mjs:66-69` names as the failure "worse than an absent one".
- D-08 (Gate position): The spend gate sits BEFORE the `route.mjs resolve`, not merely before the Task spawn. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/context.md:94-96` puts the bracket on the resolve via `--bracket-read`, and `cadence-core/bin/route.mjs:149-167` writes the dispatch half unconditionally before any resolution; `cadence-core/references/seams.md:110-113`. Confirmed on the fixture: the phase-1 analyzer `lifecycle/dispatch` and `routing/resolve` share the timestamp `11:27:05.324Z`. A gate placed after the resolve leaves a dispatch event with no worker, so every skipped phase reports an unpaired bracket - inverting the record-health signal this phase exists to sharpen, and failing acceptance criterion AC5 on its own terms.
- D-09 (Two questions, not one): `size_check` stays where it is, after `acceptance_criteria`. The spend gate is a NEW, differently-named question before `analyze` - "is this pass worth buying", not "how many plans". The four surfaces asserting "exactly one size question" restate as one size question plus one spend question: `cadence-core/workflows/context.md:259-261` (the step), `:335-337` (guardrail 3), `:352-353` (success criterion 3), `skills/cad-context/SKILL.md:19`. Evidence: `context.md:259-278` places `size_check` after the criteria explicitly "now that the criteria make size visible", and the criteria are drafted from the analyzer's own output (`:224-226`), so moving it forward strips it of the evidence its own prose says it needs, degrading the `Plan shape` line `/cad-plan` consumes (`cadence-core/workflows/plan.md:76-80`, `cadence-core/templates/CONTEXT.md:12`) for every phase.
- D-11 (No computed discriminator): The spend gate is a JUDGED ask through the ask-user seam, annotated with evidence the coordinator has already read - requirement count, surfaces the phase names, and whether a prior phase's SUMMARY deviations already settled this ground. It computes no score and holds no threshold. Evidence: measured 2026-08-12 with the operation a computed gate would perform - `planning.mjs --dir /data/code/verbatim/.planning plan-size --phase 1 --max-reqs 12` returns `requirements: 7`, `--phase 2` returns `requirements: 6`, and ROADMAP criterion 4 mandates that phase 2 DISPATCH, so a requirement-count gate orders the two fixture phases backwards. Splitting `/data/code/verbatim/.planning/ROADMAP.md` on `^### ` gives both phases 6 numbered criteria, in blocks of 1,129 B and 1,340 B - no cheap roadmap metric separates them either. With the fixture's two phases on the same side of every measurable, any threshold gets tuned until phase 2 dispatches and nothing holds it there, making criterion 1's calibration a tautology. `context.md:335-337` already bans SPIDR and splitting frameworks.

## Decisions

- D-04 (Correlation): A marker written before the phase's `phase_start` takes the phase-only correlation id and does not join the derived-id events, so the coordinator's record splits at the anchor exactly as the worker brackets already do. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:181-199` (`correlationId` falls back to `<phase>` with no anchor); measured on the fixture, phase 1's 28 events split 8 at `corr: "1"` and 20 at `corr: "1-573f325"`, which is why the 12:24:57 `cad-reviewer` dispatch is stranded in `unpaired[]`. Every `/cad-context` marker fires before any anchor, since `phase_start` is written in `/cad-execute`'s `git_guard` step - so any per-phase rollup grouping on `corr` alone reports the coordinator twice.
- D-05 (Record health is prose): "Record health" is composed in `report.md` from `unpaired`, `malformed` and `capped`; `renderTrace` emits no health object. Consuming the new events therefore means editing `cadence-core/workflows/report.md` and re-pinning its `weight-budgets.json` row in the same commit. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/report.md:43`, `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:264-275` (the `TraceRender` typedef has no health member), `cadence-core/bin/planning.mjs:2364-2382`. Without the prose edit the events reach `render`'s `events[]` and `/cad-report` never mentions them - AC3 fails while every test stays green.
- D-06 (Silence on old traces): A new `trace suggest` rule emits nothing on a trace carrying no coordinator events - never an "absent coordinator record" suggestion. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/trace-suggest.test.mjs:136` (`assert.deepEqual(suggestFromRender(render([])), [])`) and `:191` pin the empty cases by deep-equality; floors at `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace-suggest.mjs:28-31`. Otherwise every pre-existing trace - Cadence's own and the committed fixture - gains a suggestion line, which is AC1 failing.
- D-07 (Role accounting untouched): A coordinator event carries no `--role` at all, and must never carry a blank one. `roleTotals` is only touched inside the `e.family !== 'lifecycle'` guard, so a role-less marker disturbs nothing; an empty-string role keys an unnamed row that `workflows/progress.md:94-100` renders to the user as a nameless worker. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace.mjs:355`, `:370-401`; `cadence-core/bin/lib/trace-suggest.mjs:153-168` (R5 sums `roles` alone).
- D-10 (Priors widen): `load_priors` gains a read of prior phases' `SUMMARY.md`, because the "already grounded by a prior phase's deviations" arm of the gate needs evidence `context.md` never opens today - it reads `PROJECT.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md` and up to 3 prior `CONTEXT.md` files only. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/context.md:43-61` against `/data/code/verbatim/.planning/phases/1/SUMMARY.md:62-80`, which carries 6 `[deviation]` bullets under `## Deviations`, three of them correcting a `D-NN` outright; `cadence-core/workflows/report.md:41` already reads deviations from SUMMARY for its `Refuted:` line. Without this the deviations arm never fires and the gate collapses to its size arm alone.
- D-12 (Fixture committed): `/data/code/verbatim/.planning/trace.jsonl` is committed into this repo as a test fixture, unredacted. Evidence: inspected 2026-08-12 - 40 lines, 8,364 B, zero matches for `/data/` or `/home/`, and the only free-text `detail` values are `risk_surface`, `complete` and one adjudication line naming a survivor count and `claude-subagent`. Every other field is Cadence's own vocabulary (event, role, model, effort, tokens, two `.planning/`-relative read paths). Keeping it local would leave criterion 1's calibration unrunnable in CI and silently dead the day verbatim's trace is rewritten; redacting it would calibrate against numbers that are no longer the measured ones. [corrected by plan-1 deviation: the live fixture is 52 lines / 10,703 B, not 40 lines / 8,364 B - verbatim's phase 2 kept running between the measurement and the copy; the redaction claim re-verified true against the current bytes]
- D-13 (Skip arm has no fixture): The skip arm ships with no positive fixture evidence and is calibrated on the dispatch arm only; AC5 is proven against a constructed phase. Evidence: measured 2026-08-12 across `.planning/_archive-v2.{1,2,3,5,6}*/N/CONTEXT.md`, 21 phases - unique `D-NN` ids range 8 (`_archive-v2.2.0/5`, 6,478 B) to 28 (`_archive-v2.5.0/1`, 20,234 B); verbatim phase 1 is 20 ids / 12,449 B and phase 2 is 25 ids / 21,701 B. No phase in either corpus would have taken the skip arm.
- D-14 (Census counts prose): A conditional analyzer dispatch does not break the per-file bracket census, which counts WRITTEN prose lines rather than runtime dispatches - `context.md`'s row stays 1 dispatch / 1 return / 1 checkpoint as long as BOTH close arms survive the restructure. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/trace.test.mjs:728-736` (the `BRACKETING` map) and `:831-873`, which count `--bracket-read` occurrences and `--event return`/`--event checkpoint` appends in the file text; the two close arms are `cadence-core/workflows/context.md:120-124` and `:136-138`. Dropping one fails the census with "unclosed on its FAILURE path, so a worker that came back unusable goes unbilled".
- D-15 (Flag-lint table): Any new `trace append` flag is added to `self-verify`'s flag-lint contract table in the same commit as the prose that uses it. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/self-verify.mjs:211-212` lists the nine accepted flags and `:172-182` shows check 2 skips a script with no row, so the table is not optional. Otherwise the first commit carrying the marker prose turns CI red on an `unknown-flag` for a flag that exists.
- D-16 (Budget headroom): `report.md` (3,065 B) and `plan.md` (21,035 B) sit at ZERO budget headroom and `context.md` (16,194 B) has 651 B, which the spend gate's prose will exceed - so both `weight-budgets.json` rows are re-pinned in the commits that add the prose. Evidence: measured 2026-08-12 via `weighAll()` from `cadence-core/bin/lib/surface-weight.mjs` against `cadence-core/bin/weight-budgets.json:56` and `:104`; enforcement at `cadence-core/bin/self-verify.mjs:701-725`. Note the budget check is a CEILING only - `budget-undershoot` does not exist in this tree (`self-verify.mjs:695-700`, `docs/EVIDENCE.md:70-74`), so shrinking a surface is free and needs no re-pin. Note also that `prose-agreement.test.mjs:280-325` requires a consult-SITE count at each deferred-read anchor and deliberately does NOT require a measured byte figure, so no `Read` sentence this phase writes needs one.
- D-17 (Ledger drift): The four `DOCS-CLAIMS.md` rows for `context.md` are already line-stale before this phase touches anything, and are corrected as its edits shift lines. Evidence: `.planning/DOCS-CLAIMS.md:421` (CONTEXT-03) cites `context.md:76` for `config.mjs get memory.backend workflow.subagent_timeout`, but that line now reads `... get memory.backend` alone since `workflow.subagent_timeout` was deleted (`cadence-core/references/seams.md:56-60`); CONTEXT-06/07 cite line 107 for the analyzer dispatch, now at 94-105; CONTEXT-15 cites line 351 for `cursor set`, now at 297. Left uncorrected, `/cad-docs-verify` reports a wave of `stale` verdicts caused by this phase's line shifts rather than by any claim it changed.
- D-18 (Evidence page): `docs/EVIDENCE.md:80-92` states, checked 2026-08-09, that no project on this machine but Cadence has a `trace.jsonl`; the committed fixture falsifies it, and the re-pin rides this phase's commits. Evidence: that passage against `/data/code/verbatim/.planning/trace.jsonl` - 8,364 B, 40 lines, mtime 2026-08-12, spanning two phases of a non-Cadence Rust project. Left alone, the project's own evidence page denies the existence of the file its release's phase-1 criterion names as a fixture.

- [ ] AC6: Walked against verbatim phase 2's roadmap entry and its phase-1 SUMMARY deviations, the spend gate presents six named surfaces as its evidence and its recommended option is dispatch, not skip. (human-verify: needs a walked `/cad-context` run)
- [ ] AC7: `node cadence-core/bin/self-verify.mjs` and `node --test cadence-core/bin/trace.test.mjs` are both green on the commit that adds the marker prose, with no `unknown-flag`, no `budget-overrun`, and the producer census admitting the new lifecycle name.

## Flagged assumptions

- The marker event's literal name is the planner's call - `step`, `coordinator` or another - constrained only by D-02 (a fifth exported lifecycle name) and by the census reading the export rather than a copy. If wrong: a name colliding with a future worker event forces a rename across the renderer, the census and every prose surface that writes one.
- Which steps get a marker is unsettled: every step of every workflow, or only the phase-scoped ones that already bracket a worker. Likely the latter, since the residue is only meaningful between brackets. If wrong: markers on unbracketed steps produce residue figures with no bracket to subtract against, and `/cad-report` prints a coordinator time for a phase that never dispatched.
- Whether `trace render` should expose the residue as a computed field or leave the arithmetic to `report.md` follows from D-05 but is not settled by it - D-05 only establishes that the health LINE is prose. Likely a render field, so `trace suggest` and `/cad-report` cannot disagree. If wrong: the two readers compute the residue independently and drift.
- The four open `trace.mjs` defects in CAPTURE.md (`__proto__` role key, terminal-vs-dispatch role validation, `recorded` counted per event, `correlationId` colliding on an unchanged HEAD) are assumed not to block this phase. If wrong - specifically if the correlation-id collision bites a re-run during development - the marker work inherits a defect it did not cause and the plan grows a fix it did not scope.
