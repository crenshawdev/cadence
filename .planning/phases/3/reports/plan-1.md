Deviations: [deviation] Task 4's Action says to sanitize the whole raw body and then truncate. Against task 2's own new 4 MiB response ceiling that extrapolates to roughly four hours of CPU for one failure envelope, so the seam's new bound would have created a worse one. The plan's stated reason for sanitize-before-truncate (never leave half a credential in the envelope) therefore still holds, and the 1 MiB case now costs 14ms. A test pins it by wall clock with a 5s bound, which times out rather than passes if the window is removed.
