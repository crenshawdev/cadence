### B2 — Unrecognized test output is incorrectly certified as no test results

**BLOCKER — `.planning/phases/12/PLAN-2.md:83`, especially :91-92 and :96-98; `.planning/phases/12/PLAN-3.md:152`, :185-186 and Task 4 at :458-474.** The revision makes Cargo/libtest and Python unittest summary lines the exclusive production result vocabulary. Complete capture proves that all output was retained; it does not prove that unfamiliar output contains no test results.

A legitimate, explicitly named custom check can run its actual assertion, report a handwritten failure such as `answer: expected 7, received 6`, exit unsuccessfully at the red commit, and pass after the subject is corrected with unchanged test material. The specified classifier necessarily calls its failure `no results` and refuses its red/green pair. D-112 permits a confirmed relaunch only when no test results were produced, and forbids a same-plan rerun after a suite ran and failed (`CONTEXT.md:200-206`). Operator confirmation remains required, which prevents automatic retries, but does not repair the binary's false evidence classification or the explicit-command closure restriction.

**Suggested fix:** retain unrecognized output as Unknown/unclassified, not evidence of absence.
