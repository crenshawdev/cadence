# Phase 5: Recall matches the word the user typed - Summary

A Porter step-1a/1b suffix fold applied inside `tokenize()` - the one site both
indexing and querying already pass through - so `seam` finds `seams` and `close`
finds `closes`, proved against a committed 30-document fixture corpus with a
16-query named-hits baseline.

## What shipped

- The suffix fold (Porter steps 1a and 1b) - `cadence-core/bin/lib/bm25.mjs`,
  module-local `foldSuffix`, called by `tokenize` and nothing else, so
  "identical at index time and query time" is a property of there being one code
  path rather than of two sites agreeing.
- The fixture corpus and its named-hits query baseline -
  `cadence-core/bin/fixtures/recall.corpus.json` (30 documents over 4 sources,
  3 corpus tiers) and `cadence-core/bin/fixtures/recall.queries.json` (16
  queries, every `hits` list generated by running the seam against a built root).
- The ranking-regression harness and the fold's rule table -
  `cadence-core/bin/planning-recall-fold.test.mjs`, registered in the `planning`
  group via `cadence-core/bin/test.mjs` and pinned by
  `cadence-core/bin/test-groups.test.mjs`.
- The stopword-order guard - the filter still tests the RAW token, so `being`
  and `its` survive the fold instead of being deleted from the index.

## Goal check

The four commits that carry code plus their two bookends do deliver the phase
goal's stemming half, and the goal's second half was scoped out on purpose
rather than dropped. Criterion 1 holds at the tokenizer: `tokenize('seams')` and
`tokenize('seam')` both return `['seam']`, `closes`/`close` both return
`['close']`, and `named`/`naming`/`name` all return `['name']` (measured on
5d96ff44), and it holds identically at index and query time because `foldSuffix`
is module-local in `cadence-core/bin/lib/bm25.mjs` and reached only through
`tokenize`, which both paths already call. Criterion 2 holds through the
committed fixture: 30 documents and 16 queries under
`cadence-core/bin/fixtures/`, exercised by
`cadence-core/bin/planning-recall-fold.test.mjs` inside the `planning` group, and
14 of the 16 entries' named hits are unchanged across the fold with the 2 that
moved marked `changed_by_fold` in the fixture itself - the regression bar is a
committed artifact rather than a hand-run check, which is what the criterion
asked for. Criterion 3 holds by record: GH-93 items 2 and 3 are the first two
Open items above, each naming why, matching D-06. Criterion 4 holds live:
`node cadence-core/bin/test.mjs` reports 3502 tests / 3502 pass / 0 fail, and
`self-verify` reports `{"ok":true, "problems":[]}` across all 30 checks. Nothing
in the phase goal is unaccounted for; what a reader should carry forward is the
fold's stated boundary - steps 1a and 1b only, so `verifies` and `verify` are
still two terms - which is the third Open item and not a gap in what was
promised.
