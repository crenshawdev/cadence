## Durable decisions

- D-02 (Byte target): `/cad-config` lands at ~12,700 B eager, not ROADMAP criterion 2's ~11,700. The arithmetic is 20,547 - 8,052 + ~200 B of surviving `Read` sentence; the sweep applied a -43% factor to a span that is 39.2% of the eager set. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/config.md:71-133` measured at 8,052 B; `node cadence-core/bin/weight.mjs resident` (cad-config eagerBytes 20,547); `.planning/ROADMAP.md:106`; `design-notes/sweep-2026-08-10-context-weight.md:55`; `docs/EVIDENCE.md:45`. The phase's ~22,300 B aggregate still holds - gross removals measure ~23,900 B against ~1,500 B of new `Read` sentences. Chasing the stated number instead means unscoped cutting in a phase whose job is deferrals.
- D-04 (Recall gate): `references/recall.md` is read from `context.md` and `debug.md` only; `plan.md`'s two gate sites stay eager, and the `builtin`/`none` decision sentence stays eager at every site - only the `<recalled_memory>` render contract and its rationale move. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/plan.md:69-76` routes `--inline` to `inline_plan` and otherwise to `spawn_planner`, and `:98-119` and `:170-176` both carry the gate; `cadence-core/references/seams.md:235-239` counts mutually exclusive arms of ONE step as one site, and these are two steps. Costs the ~1,860 B recall recovery on `/cad-plan`. Reading two steps behind one router as one site would set that precedent for every future deferral, and moving the gate itself would force a `Read` on the `none` path just to learn the step is skipped.
- D-05 (Parallel scope): `execute.md`'s worktree-reconciliation remedy stays eager - only `execute_parallel`'s body (320-379) moves. Evidence: the remedy sits at `cadence-core/workflows/execute.md:294-311`, inside `<step name="handle_checkpoint">` (271-317), a step distinct from `execute_parallel` (319-380); `cadence-core/references/seams.md:235-239` keeps a reference consulted at more than one step eager. Moving it gives `references/execute-parallel.md` two consult sites and fails criterion 1 on the reference's own terms - the one criterion every move is graded against.
- D-06 (Template justification): `cadence-core/templates/CONTEXT.md` is justified on seams.md's SIZE term, not on branch-locality, and its `Read` sentence says so. Evidence: `cadence-core/workflows/context.md:298-344` - `<step name="write_context">` is unconditional inside `<process>`, so every `/cad-context` run reaches it; `cadence-core/references/seams.md:226-234` ("an every-path reference whose residency outweighs that single call is past break-even and defers anyway"). Criterion 1's "only some branches reach it" clause is amended for this item; leaving it unamended fails the item at the verify gate after the commit has landed.
- D-11 (Register meaning): The `stillEager` arm is vacuous for all seven new rows - none of this prose was ever an `@`-include - so each row's protection is its anchor alone, and `lib/deferred-reads.mjs`'s header is widened to state that the register also covers inlined prose promoted to a file. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/lib/deferred-reads.mjs:368-375` applies the `@`-include regex to the SKILL.md text and `:9-16` frames the register as the record of a removed include; `skills/cad-config/SKILL.md:28` includes only `workflows/config.md`. Without the widening, a maintainer reading `:9-16` correctly concludes the new rows describe no removal and deletes them - every phase-3 deferral silently unwatched, which is what the register exists against.
- D-17 (Figure enforcement): `prose-agreement.test.mjs`'s inline-byte-figure test is generalized to cover every reference whose `Read` sentence carries a measured-bytes clause. Evidence: `cadence-core/bin/prose-agreement.test.mjs:181-204` hardcodes `const REF = 'cadence-core/references/review-triggers.md'` and `for (const skill of ['cad-land', 'cad-plan-review'])`; `cadence-core/references/seams.md:239-243` mandates the figure for every deferral; `.planning/CAPTURE.md`'s open item names this drift class as one that "expands by rule". Shipping the six new `Read` sentences under the hardcoded test adds six unchecked instances of exactly the drift the sweep was written to close.

## Flagged assumptions

- `lib/deferred-reads.mjs:250-256` clears `item`/`arm` on closing a NESTED tag instead of restoring the enclosing frame's, so a `Read` sentence sitting after an editorial nested block inside a numbered process item labels `null` and files a false `deferred-read-unread`. Latent today; this phase is the first to put real rows on that grammar, so a move whose `Read` sentence lands after a nested block hits it. Recorded in `.planning/CAPTURE.md` from the phase-1 diff review.
- D-18's `DOCS-CLAIMS.md` retargeting is unenforced by any check; a missed row surfaces only at the next `/cad-docs-verify` sweep, and this phase moves six times as many rows as phase 2 did.
- D-08's coarse `check_gate` anchor means the plan-revision row is satisfiable by a `Read` sentence elsewhere in that ~120-line step. Tightening it needs a finer label form than phase 1's grammar provides, which is a grammar change rather than a row change.
- The `~22,300 B` phase total in ROADMAP and `CTW-04` rests on the sweep's arithmetic. Measured gross removals are ~23,900 B against ~1,500 B of new `Read` sentences; no criterion pins a total, so the figure is descriptive and the per-surface re-pins are what the gate reads.
